FINAL CONCLUSION
================

For the given ISBN dataset, a Binary Search Tree was constructed by inserting
the keys in the specified order.

The traversals obtained were:

Inorder:
10 20 25 30 45 50 55 60 70

Preorder:
45 20 10 30 25 60 50 55 70

Postorder:
10 25 30 20 55 50 70 60 45

The BST search required 4 comparisons for 25, 4 comparisons for 55, and
3 comparisons for 90. Linear search required 8, 9, and 9 comparisons
respectively.

Therefore, for this particular dataset, the measured number of comparisons
was lower for BST search than for linear search. However, BST performance
depends strongly on the shape and height of the tree. A balanced BST can
provide O(log n) average search time, while a skewed BST can degrade to
O(n).

Hence, the choice between BST Search and Linear Search depends on the data
structure, insertion order, tree shape, and expected number of searches.
